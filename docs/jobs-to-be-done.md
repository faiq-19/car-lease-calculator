# Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

This document establishes the **Jobs To Be Done** for the **Pakistan Islamic Car Ijarah Calculator**. 

Each job articulates the core underlying motivation and desired outcome of a specific role, following the standard format:
`When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected outcome].`

---

## Job 1: Estimate Accurate Shariah-Compliant Monthly Commitment
- **Role**: Salaried Professional
- **Job Statement**:  
  **When** I am planning to purchase a new car on monthly installments,  
  **I want to** calculate the exact monthly Ijarah rental incorporating bank profit, mandatory Takaful (Islamic insurance), and tracker subscription fees,  
  **so I can** determine whether the vehicle fits comfortably within my family's monthly budget without dealing with unexpected hidden charges.

---

## Job 2: Verify SBP Debt Burden Ratio (DBR) Affordability
- **Role**: Salaried Professional
- **Job Statement**:  
  **When** I am preparing to apply for car financing with my bank,  
  **I want to** check my monthly installment against my net monthly salary using the State Bank of Pakistan's 40% DBR benchmark,  
  **so I can** know with confidence whether my financing application will be approved before undergoing formal credit bureau (e-CIB) checks and paying non-refundable bank processing fees.

---

## Job 3: Determine Total Initial Drive-Off Cash Requirement
- **Role**: Salaried Professional / Self-Employed Businessman
- **Job Statement**:  
  **When** I am finalizing a car purchase at an automotive dealership,  
  **I want to** see a clear, itemized breakdown of all upfront costs (security deposit, bank processing charges, 16% Federal Excise Duty, and 1st month advance rental),  
  **so I can** ensure I have the exact liquid capital ready in my account on delivery day.

---

## Job 4: Bridge the SBP Rs. 3 Million Financing Cap for High-Value Vehicles
- **Role**: Self-Employed Businessman
- **Job Statement**:  
  **When** I am considering a sedan or crossover SUV priced above Rs. 4,000,000,  
  **I want to** know the exact minimum down payment required to comply with the SBP's Rs. 3,000,000 individual auto borrowing limit,  
  **so I can** structure my cash flow and avoid regulatory loan rejection.

---

## Job 5: Compare Financing Terms Across Pakistani Islamic Banks
- **Role**: Automotive Showroom Sales Consultant / Comparison Shopper
- **Job Statement**:  
  **When** a customer is weighing auto financing options between multiple financial institutions (e.g., Meezan Bank vs. BankIslami vs. Bank Alfalah),  
  **I want to** switch between pre-configured bank packages and view differences in spreads, Takaful rates, and fees,  
  **so I can** recommend the most competitive financing package for the customer's preferred vehicle and tenure.

---

## Job 6: Enforce Regulatory Engine Displacement Tenure Limits
- **Role**: Automotive Showroom Sales Consultant
- **Job Statement**:  
  **When** I am configuring financing terms for a customer,  
  **I want to** have the calculator automatically cap tenure at 3 years for vehicles above 1,000 cc and up to 5 years for vehicles 1,000 cc or below,  
  **so I can** prevent misquoting unapproved 5-year plans to customers purchasing 1.3L+ vehicles.

---

## Job 7: Maintain Custom Bank Rates Without Backend Dependencies
- **Role**: Automotive Showroom Sales Consultant
- **Job Statement**:  
  **When** a partner bank updates its benchmark spread, Takaful tier, or processing fee,  
  **I want to** update or add bank rate profiles directly in the application with instant local persistence and a one-click reset option,  
  **so I can** generate accurate quotes without requiring a code deployment or external database.
